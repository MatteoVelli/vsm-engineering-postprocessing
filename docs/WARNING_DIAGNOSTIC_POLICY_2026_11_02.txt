ASTAUTO WARNING AND DIAGNOSTIC POLICY CORRECTION
2 November 2026

A. Root cause

The original _profile_diagnostics implementation (introduced in commit 1338648)
compared every finite Energy / (SOC/100) estimate with the median using
max(0.1 kWh, 1% of the median). No documented VSM physical acceptance criterion
accompanied this screen. It ran independently of whether the requested KPIs
could be calculated. The previous battery refinement qualified its wording but
left it as a visible warning. The confirmed reference was separately classified
as visible information. _render_diagnostics therefore rendered both banners.
The initial-reference comparison reused the same unvalidated 1% assumption.

The ratio is calculated in battery.nominal_capacity_estimates_kwh;
battery_capacity_reference records its median, extrema, deviation and threshold.
Variation alone does not establish that the deterministic reference-energy
range calculation is unavailable or numerically invalid. It also does not
establish a temperature law or validate physical vehicle range.

B. Corrected policy

Energy/SOC variation and initial-reference comparisons are information with
user_visible=False. Both numerical thresholds are unchanged and explicitly
described as technical heuristics, not physical limits or KPI reliability tests.
Confirmed capacity provenance is also quiet information. Unconfirmed agro
force interpretation remains an assumption in the audit trail, rather than a
warning solely because manual physical confirmation is absent. Its inferred
and assumed statuses and qualified metric definitions remain unchanged.

Missing required channels, incompatible units, invalid required samples,
impossible battery-domain pairs and unavailable requested calculations retain
their existing warnings/errors. Unknown identity, optional battery mass and
approved unit interpretations were already quiet; they remain so. Optional
unavailable outputs remain explicitly unavailable. For all four real-source
audits the density-required warning remains visible because the requested
mass-to-volume output cannot be calculated without density. No density or
plausible replacement result is invented.

The results view reuses the collapsed Diagnostics presentation to expose all
diagnostic records, battery interpretation and source SHA-256. The same policy
records remain in Excel Metadata, manifests and all 16 PowerPoint speaker notes.
The success message and important warnings remain in the primary workflow.
