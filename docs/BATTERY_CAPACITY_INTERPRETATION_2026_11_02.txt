ASTAUTO BATTERY CAPACITY INTERPRETATION AND RANGE VALIDATION - 2 NOVEMBER 2026

A. Root-cause assessment
The legacy nominal-capacity estimate is MEDIAN(Battery Energy / (SOC/100)) over
finite samples with SOC above zero. It is NOT a fixed initial-SOC division, a
mean, or a direct nominal-capacity measurement. The diagnostic compares every
valid sample's ratio with that median; tolerance=max(0.1 kWh, 1%*abs(median)).
For Electric 03, median=346.357794394718 kWh, max deviation=3.971617369988 kWh,
tolerance=3.463577943947 kWh. The warning correctly detected ratio variation.
The unsupported part was describing that ratio as the nominal specification.
The ratio also directly influenced range, not merely diagnostics.

No arithmetic/import defect was found in Battery Energy or SOC. The variation
is observed; the data do not prove its physical cause. It is not established as
a defect in VSM, nor established as being caused solely by temperature.
The variation warning is retained at the SAME threshold with qualified wording.

B. Engineering interpretation and evidence
Sergio Rinland, ASTAUTO, 2 November 2026 confirmed a 350 kWh nominal reference
for this Electric 03 simulation and stated that nominal capacity depends on
temperature. This does NOT supply a full-cycle temperature-capacity law or an
operational lower SOC cutoff. No such law, reserve or usable SOC window is
invented. The 350 kWh value is a declared reference, not a forced identity
Energy(t)=350*SOC(t)/100 throughout the simulation.

Initial SOC=85%; initial stored energy=297.780 kWh. 350*0.85=297.500 kWh, a
0.280 kWh difference (0.094118% relative to 297.5 kWh). Energy/SOC at the initial
sample is 350.329411764706 kWh. This small reference difference is retained and
recorded, not corrected or treated as a source defect. The available evidence
does not demonstrate that display rounding alone explains it.

Over the 2,161 samples:
- SOC: 85.0000% -> 64.5782%.
- Stored energy: 297.780 -> 221.265 kWh.
- Energy/SOC equivalent: 350.329411764706 -> 342.631104614251 kWh.
- Battery cell temperature: 30.0000 -> 28.8703 degC; min/max 28.8540/30.0764.
- Exported Battery Voltage: 48.1805 -> 45.4341 V.
Both voltage and temperature vary. These co-varying measurements do not identify
the VSM SOC/energy equations, assembly scaling or a temperature-capacity law.

General battery model documentation distinguishes charge-based SOC from energy
and models voltage as a function of SOC and temperature. This explains why a
constant Energy/SOC identity must be justified, rather than assumed. It does
NOT establish which equations AVL VSM uses in this run:
[MathWorks Battery (Table-Based)](https://www.mathworks.com/help/simscape-electrical/ref/batterytablebased.html)
[MathWorks Battery (System-Level)](https://www.mathworks.com/help/sdl/ref/batterysystemlevel.html)

Range policy retained: 85% of the selected nominal-energy REFERENCE divided by
observed net stored-energy depletion per km. The existing KPI was explicitly
independent of initial SOC. Therefore a confirmed nominal reference takes
precedence over an Energy/SOC approximation. This policy, rather than proximity
to 350, justifies the new value. 85% is an energy allowance for extrapolation,
not proof that physical 85% SOC-to-cutoff usable energy equals 0.85*nominal.

Using initial stored energy instead would produce 41.7966132915 km, a different
initial-remaining-energy estimate that depends on the starting state. It does
not preserve the defined reference-energy KPI and was not introduced.
A temperature-aware or cutoff-aware actual usable range remains unvalidated.
The report continues to label range as estimated; comments, metadata, manifest,
speaker notes and UI diagnostics disclose the reference and limitations.
