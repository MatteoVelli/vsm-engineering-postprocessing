Scope

Implementation
src/vsm_postprocessing/torque_maps_presentation.py
  read_torque_map_series: resolves the Torque Maps sheet, drawing and native XY
  charts through workbook relationships. Reads each series name and numerical
  source-cell reference, including the written cached values of Excel formulas.
  It preserves original point order, signs and supplied RPM extent.
  render_torque_maps: renders four simulation scatter series plus the capability
  series actually present in each Excel chart. Orange solid peak and teal dashed
  continuous limits share compact legend entries for positive/negative curves.
  Limits include every valid point with padding; no engineering conversions,
  interpolation, extrapolation or sample reduction occur here.
  torque_maps_metadata: retains compact provenance in slide notes/manifests;
  exact numerical arrays remain in torque_maps_presentation_data.json.

src/vsm_postprocessing/continuous_torque_ppt.py
  continuous_torque_cards: selects independent per-motor maxima of existing
  maximum_segment_s, total_time_s, episode_count and maximum_utilisation.
  Utilisation is displayed as a percentage. Responsible motors and exact ties
  are retained separately for each indicator. No duration sums across motors.
  add_continuous_torque_kpi: four native compact cards for automatic results,
  one centred duration card with explicit provenance for manual fallback,
  no empty cards when results are unavailable. Valid automatic zero is retained.

src/vsm_postprocessing/profile_powerpoint_report_engine.py
  _build_template_profile_powerpoint_report: creates separate presentation-only
  map assets from the already generated workbook.
  _render_additional_plot_pair_slide: uses those assets and KPI row only on the
  Torque Maps slide; other slide rendering retains the approved path.
  _write_machine_notes: records chart sources, counts, scaling and aggregation
  rules only in Torque Maps notes. Displayed KPI count includes automatic cards.
