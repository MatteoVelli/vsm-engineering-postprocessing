2. Implementation decisions

Reuse the existing source metadata rather than reconstructing a filename from
the title or generated deck name. A shared cover helper avoids powertrain-specific
filename implementations. Preserve exact text with native PowerPoint wrapping
and Latin word splitting, rather than inserting characters into the basename.

Reuse the established tyre total rather than introducing a second formula or
adding the soft-soil components to an already inclusive total. The additional
Electric page is configured before the two soil pages; the existing renderer
numbers the resulting list dynamically. Two separate time plots provide the
same power/energy panel format as those pages and also become native Excel
charts through the existing shared plot catalogue.

Suppress Engine Load at the reporting layer rather than deleting its raw
mapping. A default-true for_report raw-channel flag controls Excel export and
the UI export chooser. Remove the associated configured statistic and plot.
Retain valid throttle on a centred, single-plot Engine Throttle page. This also
corrects legacy Diesel reporting, which inherits the same semantic definitions.

4. Requirement 2: physical definitions and numerical validation

The existing total is tyre resistance absorption, not total wheel shaft input
power and not a claim to include every possible tyre loss mechanism.

For each wheel c = FL, FR, RL, RR, use the VSM channels:
    Tyre_RollingResistancePower_c     [kW]
    Tyre_BulldozingResistancePower_c   [kW]
    Tyre_CompactionResistancePower_c   [kW]

P_total(t) = sum over corners of (P_rolling + P_bulldozing + P_compaction).
These are the existing mechanism components, not the sum of a total and its
subcomponents. The Electric_05 reference workbook explicitly sums the corner
channels at DL/DX/FL, then sums those component totals at FP. Its cached total
power unit label says kWh; the application already correctly defines power as kW.
No new native total channel was introduced or inferred from force/torque signals.
The real Electric export has no native total absorbed-power channel. It also
contains Tyre_SlipagePower_* and longitudinal/lateral tyre energy channels (kJ).
Those are outside the reference workbook's established resistance total; their
independence from its mechanisms is not established by the supplied repository.
They are therefore not added to the validated total or mixed with cumulative
energy signals. The new slide's subtitle states its three included mechanisms.

The current October profile uses the observed Track_Time timebase in seconds:
    delta_E_i = (P_(i-1) + P_i) / 2 * (t_i - t_(i-1)) / 3600 [kWh]
    delta_E_0 = 0
    E_total = cumulative sum of interval component energies.

Linearity makes this equal to trapezoidal integration of P_total. Signs are
retained; negative values are not clipped or replaced by absolute values.
Soft-soil energy comprises bulldozing plus compaction and is already included.
Nonuniform time intervals are supported. Missing/nonfinite/invalid-unit optional
constituents make the total unavailable; partial totals and replacement zeros
are not reported. Valid measured zeros remain valid.

The six cards retain the existing soil conventions: signed maximum power,
time-weighted mean power, sample RMS power, net accumulated energy change,
energy divided by the canonical distance, and canonical distance. Accumulated
energy is not integrated a second time. Unavailable cards show N/A with reasons.

legacy-profile numerical conventions are unchanged.
