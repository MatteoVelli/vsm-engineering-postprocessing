Implementation and engineering interpretation

The prior reporting inputs defaulted the rear-hitch interpretation to false,
although the established Electric/Hybrid math and Statistics sheet already
calculated the inferred load. The report-qualified card therefore displayed N/A
beside a valid historical maximum. Battery mass likewise required an explicit
configuration selection despite Sergio's verified specification. The kg/m result
was only stored in a trace, and remaining-load charts retained discharge labels.

The existing reporting profile YAML now stores reusable machine configurations.
Inheritance merges configuration records by configuration_id; the ordinary
strict profile parser validates their inputs, identity lists and powertrains.
There is no separate machine registry or replacement configuration system.
A source must match a model name/alias, a configuration token and a compatible
powertrain. Configuration mass alone never identifies a machine. Source suffixes
are irrelevant. A contradictory filename powertrain prevents automatic matching.
A cosmetic report-title override preserves the actual source identification.
An explicit advanced configuration must be compatible with model and powertrain.

RoboSprayer / RoboSprayer V5 + 3500kg + Electric selects the Sergio-confirmed
470 kg pack. Full Size / Full Size Sprayer + 8500kg + Electric or Hybrid selects
~1700 kg, explicitly estimated; the provenance says approximately 1700 kg or more
and calculation ongoing. Full Size 8500kg Diesel selects its separate inferred
load mapping and receives no electric pack. Caiman SP 9300kg Hybrid has a known
load mapping but no verified pack specification. Unmatched machines retain
Not specified and cannot acquire load interpretation from their mass alone.
Battery specifications affect reporting only, never vehicle or energy dynamics.

The mapping supports an INFERRED load; it does not certify that a dynamic hitch
force is a direct measurement of chemical mass. Repository workbook formulas and
historical Sergio source mappings support that interpretation for the identified
profiles. No source model definition independently proves sensor physics or
chemical density. The reports retain the inferred status and provenance.

Definitions and shared calculation

agro_reporting.calculate_reporting_kpis resolves semantic raw sources and invokes
one report calculator. The resulting object and shared reporting_metric_rows
registry supply both Excel and PowerPoint. Presentation code never recomputes
chemical quantities. Reporting provenance stays in Statistics, hidden workbook
metadata, report manifests and PowerPoint notes, without diagnostic JSON on slides.

Remaining agrochemical mass [kg] = -rear hitch vertical force [N] / 9.81.
Agro Capacity [kg] = maximum observed inferred remaining mass, NOT manufacturer
nominal tank capacity. Nominal tank capacity remains unspecified.
Discharged mass [kg] = initial remaining mass - final remaining mass over a valid
continuous full report window. It differs from both remaining mass and capacity.
Mass discharge diagnostic [kg/m] = discharged mass / (last distance - first distance).
Agro Discharge Rate [L/m] = discharged volume / that same travelled distance.
This is a full drive-cycle average, including stationary/non-spraying intervals;
it does not silently become an active-spraying rate. There is no verified spraying
mask or operating-phase segmentation supporting a different default denominator.

Volume priority is explicitly trusted cumulative volume in L (last minus first),
then explicitly trusted volumetric flow integrated by the trapezoidal rule over
observed, strictly increasing time in seconds, then supported mass divided by an
explicit positive finite density in kg/L. Flow supports L/s, L/min and L/h.
The initial time interval is not extrapolated. Invalid configured direct sources
remain unavailable rather than silently switching methods. Fuel-named channels
are refused as agro volume/flow sources. No default density is supplied.

Distance supports m/km and must be finite, nonnegative, nondecreasing and have a
positive endpoint difference. Nonfinite/insufficient force, unit errors, all-zero
force, negative inferred contents, refill/loading increases and discontinuities
prevent an unsupported mass-derived rate. No negative loads are clamped. Isolated
jumps greater than 20 times the median positive increment, with at least five
positive increments, trigger a conservative discontinuity warning. This screen
is not a proven segmentation algorithm. Multiple monotonic discharge phases and
plateaus are accepted over the full window; refills need a separately validated
per-cycle analysis. A positive observed maximum may remain an inferred capacity
estimate even when later contents are negative; the quality warning is retained.

Electric/Hybrid legacy math uses 9.81. Diesel's historical math uses 9.80665 and
is preserved exactly, including its bottom statistics and plotted samples. Its
legacy mass header/axes explicitly identify that conversion. The new reporting
capacity and mass/volume rate consistently use Sergio's 9.81 convention. The
manifest records both the reporting gravity and preserved legacy expression.
These historical conversion outputs are not relabelled as a second new capacity
KPI. All captured arrays/statistics/KPIs are compared exactly against baseline.

Interface and reporting

Upload identifies the powertrain from source identity and selects the profile.
A read-only caption shows detected configuration, battery specification and load
interpretation. The user validates and generates reports. The existing profile
choice remains editable and zero/inactive signals never determine powertrain.
Uncommon battery, density, physical interpretation and exact source mappings are
inside a collapsed Advanced reporting overrides section. The normal workflow
passes no override dictionary that could erase the automatic configuration.
Existing channel/statistic/plot selections and source-quality validation remain.

Electric/Hybrid slide 5 displays pack mass as editable text. Slide 8 includes
separate editable AGRO CAPACITY and AGRO DISCHARGE RATE cards, a capacity qualifier,
and a valid kg/m diagnostic or a readable unavailable reason. Full Size Diesel
adds its agro cards to Executive Results (slide 3); its slide 8 remains throttle.
The Diesel explanation replaces the old template note to avoid overlap and uses
light text on the dark background. Relevant chart titles/axes now say Remaining
Agrochemical Mass; legacy IDs, aliases and raw source names stay traceable.
Excel summaries contain live Statistics references, with the same values/units,
status, provenance and explicit N/A reasons. Raw N and calculated kg remain distinct.
