VSM TYRE ROLLING RESISTANCE CORRECTION - 18 OCTOBER 2026

ROOT CAUSE AND DEPENDENCIES

The Electric base profile defined tyre_total_resistance_power as rolling +
compaction + bulldozing, and likewise added all three interval energies.
Hybrid inherited those definitions. Sergio's clarification establishes that
the rolling channels already contain both soil components, so those additions
counted the diagnostic components twice.

calculate_profile_math_channels evaluates the profile expressions and their
dependencies. calculate_profile_statistics consumed the additive accumulated
energy through tyre_total_energy_accumulated_last; that statistic also feeds
total_energy_consumption_tyres_aux. The tyres_energy_consumption plot used the
additive power and energy in Excel and PowerPoint. The October Electric profile
also added a tyre_total detail slide, two single-series plots and six cards,
whose sources were selected in build_soil_powerpoint_metrics.

Diesel has independent profiles and already uses rolling resistance directly.
It has no additive tyre-total channels or measured bulldozing/compaction inputs.
No Diesel calculations, inputs or profiles were changed.

CORRECTION

Removed these four MATH definitions from the common Electric/Hybrid base:
  tyre_total_resistance_power
  tyre_total_resistance_energy_kwh
  tyre_total_resistance_energy_wh
  tyre_total_resistance_energy_accumulated

No duplicate aliases or replacement integration calculations were introduced.
Authoritative sources are total_rolling_resistance_power [kW] and
tyre_rolling_resistance_energy_accumulated [kWh]. Existing rolling interval kWh,
Wh and accumulated kWh are preserved without numerical changes. Each profile's
existing timebase, signed integration, initial-sample treatment and units remain
unchanged. The October profiles retain observed-interval trapezoidal integration;
the legacy profiles retain their existing validated sample_energy_kwh convention.

Historical template rows for the four retired channels are skipped by normalized
source/report identity and MATH type, never by Excel column letter. Other strict
template mismatch checks remain active. Chart references and live KPI formulas
continue to resolve the newly positioned columns through semantic identities.

Both combined consumption series now use the rolling channels. The Electric
detail page is adapted to "Tyre Rolling Resistance Power & Energy" and explicitly
states that rolling resistance includes bulldozing and compaction. Its six card
IDs, two plot IDs, geometry and formatting are retained for compatibility; it
does not present an additional independent tyre loss. Soil force, power, energy,
plots and component-specific KPIs are preserved.

KPI AUDIT (IDENTIFIERS AND UNITS PRESERVED)

tyre_total_energy_accumulated_last [kWh]: LAST of accumulated rolling energy.
total_energy_consumption_tyres_aux [kWh]: that corrected statistic plus the
  existing auxiliary_energy_accumulated_max statistic, with the existing
  profile-specific auxiliary definition unchanged.
Electric presentation-only cards:
  tyre_total_max_power [kW]: MAX(total_rolling_resistance_power).
  tyre_total_mean_power [kW]: existing time-weighted mean of rolling power.
  tyre_total_rms_power [kW]: existing all-sample RMS of rolling power.
  tyre_total_total_energy [kWh]: LAST(rolling accumulated) - FIRST(rolling accumulated).
  tyre_total_energy_per_km [kWh/km]: corrected net energy / distance_km_last.
  tyre_total_distance [km]: unchanged existing distance_km_last.
The pre-existing total_rolling_resistance_power_max statistic was already correct
and is unchanged. Battery-derived consumption/range, fuel, auxiliary and soil
diagnostic metrics are unchanged. No new engineering KPI formulas were added.
